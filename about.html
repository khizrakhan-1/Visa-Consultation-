Who We Are

Visa Klub is a dedicated international education and student visa consultancy based in Pakistan. We help students explore study opportunities, navigate university admissions, and prepare for student visas in the UK, USA, Australia, China, Romania, Georgia, and Cyprus.

Our student-focused approach combines expert counseling, transparent guidance, and end-to-end support — from your first consultation to your departure. We believe in empowering students with clear, honest information at every step of the journey.

In addition to admissions and visa support, we offer IELTS and PTE preparation through professional online and on-campus classes to help students meet English language requirements with confidence.
