IELTS Course Curriculum

Comprehensive preparation across all four test modules.

Listening

Understanding conversations, identifying key information, note-taking, multiple-choice, matching, form completion, and listening strategies.

Reading

Skimming, scanning, identifying keywords, understanding passages, matching headings, True/False/Not Given, and time management.

Writing

Task 1: data description, structure, vocabulary. Task 2: essay structure, argument development, coherence, grammar, and vocabulary.

Speaking

Interview, individual long turn, discussion, pronunciation, fluency, vocabulary, grammar, confidence-building, and mock interviews.
