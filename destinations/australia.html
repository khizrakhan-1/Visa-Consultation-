Admission Requirements

General guidelines for applying to Australian universities.

Academic Requirements

High school diploma for undergraduate; bachelor's degree for graduate programs.

English Language

IELTS (typically 6.0–7.0), PTE, or TOEFL scores.

Required Documents

Transcripts, SOP, Letters of Recommendation, and application forms.

Financial Documents

Proof of funds to cover tuition and living expenses.
