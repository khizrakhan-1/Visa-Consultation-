Admission Requirements

General guidelines for applying to US universities. Requirements vary by institution.

Academic Requirements

High school diploma for undergraduate; bachelor's degree for graduate programs. Competitive CGPA/GPA.

English Language

TOEFL, IELTS, PTE, or Duolingo scores depending on university requirements.

Required Documents

Transcripts, SOP, Letters of Recommendation, and application forms.

Financial Documents

Proof of funds to cover tuition and living expenses (I-20 requirements).

Step-by-step guidance for your US student visa journey.

01

Choose Program

02

University Application

06

Attend your visa interview at the US Embassy.

07
