Admission Requirements

General guidelines for applying to UK universities. Requirements vary by institution.

Academic Requirements

High school diploma for undergraduate; bachelor's degree for graduate programs.

English Language

IELTS (typically 6.0–7.5), PTE, or equivalent English test scores.

Required Documents

Transcripts, SOP, Letters of Recommendation, and application forms.

Financial Documents

Proof of funds for tuition and living expenses.

UK Student Visa Process

Step-by-step guidance for your UK Student Visa (formerly Tier 4).

01

Choose Program

Select a licensed UK university and program.

02

University Application

Submit applications and required documents.

03

04

Prepare Documents

Gather financial proof and academic documents.

05

Apply Online

06

Biometrics

07

Plan your travel and arrival in the UK.
